Publications论文

  • 2026
    Neural Non-Equilibrium Hamiltonian Monte Carlo for Corrected Boltzmann Sampling
    arXiv

    Learned stochastic Hamiltonian paths with recorded-work correction: path-SNIS/path-IMH plus a proved shared-bridge round-trip Metropolis kernel. Tested on analytic many-well and finite-volume 2D phi^4 targets; molecular and compact U(1) results retain explicit feasibility boundaries.用记录功修正可学习的随机 Hamiltonian 路径：包括 path-SNIS / path-IMH，以及经过证明的共享桥接点 round-trip Metropolis 核。已在解析多阱与有限体积二维 phi^4 上测试；分子和紧致 U(1) 结果保留明确的可行性边界。

  • 2026
    Stochastic Path Sampler For Lattice Field Theory
    arXiv

    Data-free neural sampler based on nonequilibrium thermodynamics and stochastic quantization; matches HMC quality with much shorter autocorrelation in the pseudocritical region of 2D phi^4. Co-first author with S. Chen.基于非平衡热力学与随机量子化的数据无关神经采样器；在 2D φ⁴ 伪临界区，质量与 HMC 相当，自相关时间显著更短。与 S. Chen 共同一作。

  • 2026
    Operator Spectroscopy of Trained Lattice Samplers
    arXiv

    Operator-spectroscopy framework that probes what neural samplers (flow / diffusion / autoregressive) actually learn about lattice field theories.用算符谱学的框架去探测神经采样器（flow / diffusion / 自回归）究竟学到了什么样的格点场论结构。

  • 2025
    Variational Autoregressive Networks Applied to $\phi^4$ Field Theory Systems
    arXiv

    RL-style data-free training of variational autoregressive networks for the Ising model and 2D phi^4; single-site and block Metropolis-Hastings corrections reduce residual bias of pure VAN sampling.用强化学习方式数据无关地训练变分自回归网络，应用于 Ising 模型与 2D φ⁴；单点 / 块 Metropolis-Hastings 修正显著降低纯 VAN 采样的剩余偏差。
