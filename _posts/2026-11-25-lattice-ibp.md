Lattice Monte Carlo struggles with critical slowing down, topological freezing and the sign problem. This post describes a route without sampling: integration-by-parts relations reduce lattice integrals to a finite set of master integrals, whose differential equations in the hopping parameter give exact results to arbitrary precision on small lattices, used here to test complex Langevin and other samplers.
